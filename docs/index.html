Unitree G1-D · digital twin

G1-D Autonomy Stack

SLAM navigation, A* planning and a GR00T flow-matching VLA composed into one loop: navigate to the table, bimanual pick, carry across the room, place — behind a human accept gate and a LiDAR failsafe, validated in a MuJoCo twin before touching hardware.

29-DoF G1 · force-reactive walk

Force-Reactive Locomotion

A 29-DoF G1 walks where it is commanded while its arms react to forces it has no sensor for. A small MLP infers per-hand wrench from proprioception alone; splitting the regression into separate direction and magnitude heads roughly tripled magnitude R². One policy covers carrying and pushing.

Pedipulation · swing-foot tracking

Single-Leg Pedipulation

Balancing on one leg with the arms frozen around a payload, pushing an obstacle clear with the free foot. The actor sees only on-robot-computable observations, enforced by a deployability gate — 0.993 deterministic survival with zero torque saturation.
